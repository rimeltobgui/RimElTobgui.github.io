From Workstation to Edge at IEEE MetroSea and Global Ocean Robotics Day

We presented From Workstation to Edge at IEEE MetroSea 2026 and at Global Ocean Robotics Day at Sorbonne University Abu Dhabi. We compared the same reef-monitoring tasks on a workstation and a Raspberry Pi 4. On the Pi, generating a language response took more time than segmenting the reef image.
