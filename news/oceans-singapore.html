Benchmarking multi-object trackers at OCEANS Singapore

Our OCEANS 2024 paper compares lightweight detectors and multi-object trackers on two datasets, including the underwater BrackishMOT dataset. We benchmarked the combinations on a GPU, a CPU, and a Raspberry Pi 4 to assess their suitability for onboard tracking.
